Why Local Open-Weight Models Democratize Botanical Micro-Businesses

1. Supplier Privacy

Boutique terrarium builders source rare bryophytes and glassware. Running inference locally on Ollama keeps exact BOMs, supplier lists, and profit margins safely on the maker's laptop.

2. Zero Marginal Cost

Sellers operate in greenhouses or outdoor markets without reliable Wi-Fi. An open-weight Llama-3.2 or Qwen-2.5 model runs offline at $0/month, protecting craft margins.

3. Biological Guardrails

General LLMs hallucinate plant care (e.g. sealing desert succulents in 90% humidity glass). We enforce a deterministic biological engine before passing parameters to the model schema.
